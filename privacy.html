Neuroictal Privacy Policy

Neuroictal stores your name, emergency contacts, wait settings, alarm preferences, seizure notes, and fall logs on this phone. When a fall alert fires, SMS may include your name, time, and an optional map link from your last known location.

Contacts permission is only used when you pick people. Phone state helps send multi-part SMS on some devices. Data is not sold. Backup files you export are under your control.

SMS, location, contacts, and phone state stay on this device for alerts and setup. Neuroictal does not upload your health notes to a Neuroictal cloud.

Neuroictal is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent seizures. Emergency services are never dialed automatically — only the contacts you choose are texted.

For Play Data safety: declare SMS, location, contacts, and phone permissions as app functionality.
